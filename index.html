Context from AGC (Oct 15, 2026)

The Associated General Contractors of America reports its producer price index for inputs to new nonresidential construction rose 11.9% from September 2025 to September 2026, the most in four years. It cites steel mill products up 27.6% and copper and brass mill shapes up 30.0% year over year, with price increases spreading beyond fuel and metals. AGC release

Subject: Copper up 30% on the year. What to do about your bids

Morning,

Here's your Monday read on what building materials are doing. Latest BLS producer price data is for Sep 2026.

The headline

The AGC says construction input costs rose 11.9% from September 2025 to September 2026, the biggest jump in four years, with metals and fuel leading it. Steel mill products are up 27.6% and copper and brass mill shapes about 30.0%.

The board

  • Lumber (all): 282.3 (-1.4% on the month, +8.7% on the year). Details
  • Framing (softwood lumber): 288.6 (-2.0% on the month, +14.2% on the year). Details
  • Windows & doors (wood): 218.9 (+0.1% on the month, +3.8% on the year). Details
  • Roofing (asphalt shingle): 370.4 (-1.8% on the month, +3.7% on the year). Details
  • Cabinets (wood kitchen): 290.9 (+1.0% on the month, +5.4% on the year). Details
  • Concrete (ready-mix): 395.3 (+0.0% on the month, +2.3% on the year). Details
  • Copper (brass mill shapes): 835.3 (+1.4% on the month, +30.0% on the year). Details

What to do this week

  1. Plumbing and HVAC bids: shorten your copper quote window and add an escalation line.
  2. Framing: softwood has eased the last two months but is still above last year. Get a fresh yard quote before you commit to a number.
  3. Roofing: diesel is up sharply, and shingles follow petroleum. Keep quotes short-dated.

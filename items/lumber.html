What this means for your next bid

Lumber is the biggest swing item in a framing package. Producer prices are up on the year, so don't carry an old quote. Price the lumber package as close to the order date as you can, and put a short price-hold (7-14 days) and an escalation clause in the bid if you can't buy at signing. Ask your yard for a locked quote on the full package, not just the first few loads.

Editorial note written from the data above; general guidance, not a quote. Check with your own supplier.

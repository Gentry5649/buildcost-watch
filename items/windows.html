What this means for your next bid

Window and door prices have risen steadily and slowly rather than spiking. The bigger bid risk is lead time, not price: confirm the quote's validity date and the delivery window with your supplier, and get the order in early on custom or large-format units. Add a modest allowance for increases between bid and order.

Editorial note written from the data above; general guidance, not a quote. Check with your own supplier.

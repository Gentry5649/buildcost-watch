What this means for your next bid

Ready-mix is the calmest item on this page: flat month to month and up only modestly on the year. Local plant pricing and delivery charges still vary a lot, so confirm per-yard price, fuel surcharges and short-load fees with your plant. Rebar and mesh are steel, and steel is far more volatile than concrete itself.

Editorial note written from the data above; general guidance, not a quote. Check with your own supplier.

What this means for your next bid

Copper is the fastest mover here. AGC reports copper and brass mill shapes up about 30% year over year as of September 2026. For plumbing and HVAC rough-in bids, get a firm supplier quote right before you bid, shorten how long your price is valid, and add an escalation clause or an allowance for copper tube and fittings.

What this means for your next bid

Studs, joists and rafters are softwood lumber, so this is the number to watch for the framing line. Softwood is running above where it was a year ago but has eased the last two months. Check current yard pricing before you lock a framing number, and avoid quoting a framing package more than a couple of weeks out without a price-adjustment clause.

Editorial note written from the data above; general guidance, not a quote. Check with your own supplier.

What this means for your next bid

Cabinet prices are up but moving slowly. Cabinets are usually quoted well before delivery, so the risk is a price increase between your allowance and the order. Get the cabinet quote in writing with an expiration date, and tell the client the allowance is an estimate until the order is placed.

Editorial note written from the data above; general guidance, not a quote. Check with your own supplier.

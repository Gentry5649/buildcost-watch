What this means for your next bid

Shingles and underlayment track petroleum, and diesel prices are up sharply this year. Keep roofing quotes short-dated, ask your supplier about price protection on large orders, and consider a fuel/material escalation line on longer jobs. Don't reuse a roofing number from last spring.

Editorial note written from the data above; general guidance, not a quote. Check with your own supplier.

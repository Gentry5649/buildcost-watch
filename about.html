About BuildCost Watch

A simple, free price tracker for people who build and remodel homes.

Material costs can change a bid between the day you write it and the day you buy. BuildCost Watch pulls public government price data for the materials that matter most, shows which way each one is moving, and says in plain English what it means for your next bid.

Where the numbers come from

All figures are U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Producer Price Indexes, retrieved through the St. Louis Fed's FRED service. They are index values, not dollars per board foot or per yard. They show direction and size of change, and they lag the market by a few weeks. We also cite published figures from AGC when relevant, with links.

What it isn't

It is not a quote, not a forecast, and not financial advice. Your supplier's price is the price.

About the author

I'm Mike Gentry, owner of WIT Construction, LLC in St. Louis, Missouri. We are in the home remodeling business. We provide roofing, siding, decks, room additions, kitchens, baths, basements and the occasional custom home.

I started BuildCostWatch because I buy these materials every week and wanted one simple place to see which way prices are moving before I bid the next job. In the future I plan to add labor costs as well. Eventually, I want to provide full project costs based on square footage pricing and do it by region of the country.

I hope that you will find this a useful tool for preparing your next proposal.
